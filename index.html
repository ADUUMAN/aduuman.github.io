What it does

Cut & fill volumes

Compare design, previous and current surfaces from LandXML or GeoTIFF DEMs and get cut and fill volumes along the alignment.

Cross-sections & DXF

Sections at any station step with profiles, cut/fill areas, offset and height tables, and slope labels, exported as DXF ready for AutoCAD.

Batch project scanning

Point it at your folders: it detects surfaces, alignments, dates and roles, proposes projects and runs them all in one go.

Largest-change sections

One merged DXF for all projects, with the sections that changed the most framed and ranked so you review them first.

Reports & summaries

HTML and PDF reports, one-page summaries, charts, maps and key sections for monthly progress submissions.

Persian & English

Interface and reports in both languages, with quality checks on the input data before volumes are trusted.

How it works

  1. Scan. Choose the folders with your surfaces and alignments.
  2. Review. The toolkit proposes projects and flags anything uncertain for you to confirm.
  3. Run. Volumes, sections and reports are produced for every project.
  4. Deliver. Open the merged DXF and the reports, starting from the sections with the biggest change.
